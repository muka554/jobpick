Editorial standards and corrections policy

By Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team · Last updated: August 25, 2026 · Informational guidance

Middle East Job Hub is an independent information site. We help visitors organize job searches and connect to external job platforms; we do not recruit, place candidates, sell vacancies, or guarantee outcomes.

What we publish

We publish practical job-search guidance, country and city resources, platform explanations, scam-awareness material, and links to public job-search destinations. Our aim is to help readers make a more informed next step, not to replace an employer, recruiter, lawyer, government authority, or regulated advisor.

We avoid presenting external vacancy listings as our own content. Where we link to a platform, that platform remains responsible for its listing, availability, application process, and privacy practices.

How we review platform links

We review a platform’s public destination, country relevance, visible search route where available, and whether a link is appropriate for the category in which it appears. We may use a direct search URL, a direct platform page, or an official public portal.

Platform availability can change without notice. A working link does not mean that every role is current or that every employer on a platform has been independently verified by us.

Corrections and updates

We welcome reports of broken links, outdated descriptions, misleading claims, or accessibility issues. Use the contact page and include the page URL, a short explanation, and any public evidence that helps us review the issue.

We aim to correct factual or technical issues promptly, but we do not guarantee a fixed response time. Materially changed guidance is updated with a visible revision date. Material factual, source, editorial-scope, and policy amendments are also recorded in our public corrections log; ordinary layout, accessibility, and technical maintenance is not normally listed unless it changes a reader-facing factual or policy statement.

Editorial independence and advertising

Advertising or referral relationships do not determine our guide conclusions. Any paid relationship, sponsored placement, or affiliate arrangement should be disclosed clearly. Readers should not treat advertising as an endorsement of an employer, recruiter, or job offer.

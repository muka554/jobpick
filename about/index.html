About

What Gulf Job Hub is

Gulf Job Hub is a free search-aggregator page for people looking for work in the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. Instead of opening ten different job sites one at a time, you type a role once here and get direct search links into each platform's own results.

What this site is not

Gulf Job Hub does not host, store, or control job listings itself. It does not scrape, republish, or claim ownership of content from linked platforms.

Who runs it

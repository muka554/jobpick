About

What Middle East Job Hub is

Middle East Job Hub is a free search-aggregator page for people looking for work in the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. Instead of opening ten different job sites one at a time, you type a role once here and get direct search links into each platform's own results — Bayt, LinkedIn, Indeed, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, and around a dozen more general, Gulf-specialist, recruitment-agency, and national-employment sources.

What this site is not

Middle East Job Hub does not host, store, or control any job listings itself. It does not scrape, republish, or claim ownership of content from the platforms it links to. Every search takes you to that platform's own site, under that platform's own terms. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any of the job boards, recruitment agencies, or government portals listed.

Think of this page as a well-organized bookmarks list with a smart search box attached — not a job board in its own right.

Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team

Last reviewed: August 26, 2026 · Publisher and guide-maintenance profile

Brand note: Middle East Job Hub is the publisher of jobpick20.com, and JobPick is the name used for its interactive job-search experience.

Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team maintains the site’s city and country guides, platform explanations, and job-search safety material. The team does not recruit, place candidates, sell vacancies, or guarantee any third-party listing or employment outcome.

How the team works

Guides are revised when there is a material source, route, content, or user-experience change. Each materially revised guide shows a visible review date and a concise description of what changed. We do not update dates merely for appearance.

Source approach. Public-route and regulated-process claims are linked to the relevant government, public authority, official employer, or other authoritative source. Editorial planning advice is labelled as editorial judgment and is not presented as a labour-market forecast, legal advice, eligibility decision, or hiring guarantee.

Corrections and transparency

Readers can report a broken link, inaccurate statement, or accessibility issue through the contact page. Include the page URL and public evidence where available. Material corrections are reflected in the relevant guide’s visible review note. Read the full editorial standards and corrections policy and the advertising disclosure for more detail.

What this page does not claim

This author profile does not certify individual vacancies, employers, recruiters, legal rights, immigration eligibility, work permits, account ownership, advertising approval, or search-engine indexing. Readers should confirm current requirements directly with the relevant employer or authority.

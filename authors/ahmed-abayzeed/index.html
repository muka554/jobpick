Publisher profile

Ahmed Abayzeed

Publisher and editorial lead, Middle East Job Hub · Last reviewed: August 27, 2026

Ahmed Abayzeed is the named publisher of Middle East Job Hub, the organization behind jobpick20.com. JobPick is the site’s interactive job-search experience; Middle East Job Hub publishes and maintains the associated editorial resources.

Editorial role and accountability

Publisher oversightResponsible for the site’s editorial scope, publisher disclosures, and separation of informational guidance from third-party vacancy content.
Source and update reviewOversees the review of material source changes, platform-route changes, and reader-reported issues before substantive corrections are published.
Reader contactProvides a visible route for readers to raise factual, source, accessibility, or policy concerns through the site contact page.

What this profile does and does not claim

This page identifies editorial accountability. It does not claim that Ahmed Abayzeed or Middle East Job Hub recruits candidates, certifies vacancies, verifies individual employers, provides legal or immigration advice, assesses eligibility, or guarantees a hiring outcome. Individual employment, visa, contract, and licensing questions should be directed to the relevant employer, public authority, or qualified adviser.

How guide bylines work

Substantive country, city, platform, and safety guides identify the Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team byline. That byline represents the site’s editorial maintenance process, including source review, reader-correction handling, and visible revision notes. It does not imply that every guide has been reviewed by a named external professional or government authority.

Sources, corrections, and contact

Readers can review the site’s editorial standards, see material amendments in the public corrections log, and report an issue through the contact page. A useful report includes the page URL, the statement or link that needs review, and public evidence where available.

Transparency note: This publisher profile intentionally does not add unverified career history, credentials, affiliations, testimonials, or a personal photograph. It will be expanded only with factual information approved by the named publisher.

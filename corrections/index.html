Corrections log

22 November 2026: six public employment-route walkthroughs

Added dated public journeys to the employment-route comparison. Corrected the earlier gateway-only FAHR account implication: a visitor search was reachable without registering. Recorded the sampled Dubai Careers login boundary, Bayt registration/privacy choices, Hays automatic-account notice, Accor first-form hand-off and DHL unavailable application destination. These are bounded observations, not claims that every vacancy follows the same flow. No candidate data, account, CV upload, payment or application was submitted. Sample job references are historical route evidence, not vacancy recommendations.

22 November 2026: support and customer-service practice

Added fictional task packs, worked responses and interview follow-ups to the technical-support and customer-service guides. Removed unsupported claims about market concentration, copied listings, scam frequency and standard interview behaviour, and replaced unlabelled numerical CV examples with clearly labelled practice evidence. The exercises contain no real incident, customer, refund, production result or employer assessment. Search prompts and training rules are now explicitly editorial rather than market facts or legal rules.

22 November 2026: complete CV application workshop

Expanded the CV toolkit with a fictional vacancy, evidence map, complete CV, supporting statement, email alternative and blank printable workbook. All candidate details and outcomes are labelled as invented teaching material, not real reader reports. Clarified the difference between Greenhouse upload and parsing limits, preserved tool limits and added no universal ATS pass claim. No application was sent and no recruiter result was measured.

22 November 2026: employer-check evidence and worked cases

Substantially revised the UAE employer-verification guide to separate company, contact, vacancy and offer evidence. Removed unsupported quick-check assurances and the suggestion that JobPick employer feeds are verified. Added three explicitly fictional worked cases, a next-action record, authority limits and an observed public MOHRE inquiry route. No real company, agency or individual was investigated in those examples.

22 November 2026: city-guide evidence limits

Removed unsupported hiring-share, district concentration and search-productivity claims from the Amman, Cairo, Doha, Kuwait City, Manama, Muscat guides. Corrected the HIA careers route and limited the BAC reference to that company rather than all airport jobs. Removed citations that did not support their attached claims. Retained practical application checks, labelled editorial search prompts and honest source-review dates.

22 November 2026: country-guide evidence limits

Corrected the Oman guide to distinguish Omanisation percentages from occupation restrictions, employer authorisation and worker permits. Removed unsupported hiring-cycle and eligibility inferences in the Kuwait, Jordan, Bahrain, Egypt and UAE starter guides. These pages no longer infer interview delays, market-wide hiring patterns, universal CV requirements or individual legal status from general sources. Replaced those statements with practical, labelled editorial checks. Earlier source-check dates remain visible rather than being presented as new verifications.

22 November 2026: Qatar employer-change guidance

Corrected the Qatar guide: it wrongly said employer changes generally required an NOC or completion of a minimum contract term. Qatar government and ILO guidance confirms removal of the NOC requirement. The guide now distinguishes that reform from notice periods and the official employer-change process, with links to the sources checked.

21 November 2026: storage and listing scope

Corrected the homepage footer in English, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu: recent searches, favourites and application records can be saved in the browser, search-history sync is optional and account-based, and optional Google Analytics is consent-gated. Removed the inaccurate blanket claim that nothing is stored.

The About page and platform methodology now distinguish directory links from selected public employer feeds, and the account-based upload workspace from standalone local tools. This corrects the description of existing behaviour; it does not add storage, tracking or uploads.

Maintained by Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page records material factual, source, editorial-scope, and policy corrections to Middle East Job Hub guidance. It does not list ordinary layout, accessibility, or technical maintenance unless that work changes a reader-facing factual or policy statement.

How to report an issue. Send the page URL, the statement or link you believe needs review, and public supporting evidence where available through the contact page. We assess reports against the original source and update the affected guide or policy when a material correction is warranted.

What this log does not do

A corrections log cannot verify every external vacancy, employer, recruiter, service condition, or application outcome. External platform content can change after publication. Readers should use the original employer or official public-authority source for current instructions and should report a specific issue when they find one.

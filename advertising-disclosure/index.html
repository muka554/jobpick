Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Current advertising status

Middle East Job Hub is free to use. Google AdSense code is limited to substantive editorial guides and city guides. The interactive job-search hub, country and city selectors, platform directory, saved searches, Private Application Tracker, account interface, and sign-in features do not load Google AdSense code. At present, no sponsored listing or affiliate placement is active inside the interactive job-search hub or platform results. Any future commercial relationship will be labelled clearly and described on this page before it is used.

How advertising is limited and marked

Advertising is limited to substantive editorial and city-guide pages, not the interactive search hub, country/city filters, or platform directory. It must be clearly labelled “Advertisement,” visually separated from editorial content, and never styled as a job-platform row, search result, or navigation control.

No editorial influence

Advertisers do not influence which job platforms are listed, how they're categorized, or how search links are built. The list of job platforms reflects an editorial judgment about what's useful for job seekers in the UAE, independent of any advertising relationship.

Google AdSense and consent

For visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s consent message is used where applicable to provide advertising and related analytics choices. The site’s Privacy choices control opens Google’s consent-revocation flow on pages using that message. Read the Privacy Policy for the current data-use information and Google’s Business Data Responsibility information for Google’s use of data.

Affiliate or referral links

At present, the job-board links on this site are not affiliate or referral links, and we do not receive a commission when you click them or apply through them. If that changes for any platform in the future, we will name the platform, identify the commercial relationship, and disclose whether a commission may be earned. Commercial relationships will not determine factual descriptions, source accuracy, or editorial conclusions. See the Partnerships page for the current employer and sponsorship principles.

Questions

If you're unsure whether something on this site is an ad, a sponsored link, or an unpaid editorial link, feel free to ask — see the Contact page.

Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Current advertising status

Middle East Job Hub is free to use. As of August 25, 2026, the site does not currently display Google AdSense units, sponsored links, affiliate placements, or paid placement inside the interactive job-search hub or platform results.

How any future advertising would be marked

If advertising is introduced in the future, it will appear only on substantive editorial pages, not on the interactive search hub, country/city filters, or platform directory. It will be clearly labelled “Advertisement,” visually separated from editorial content, and never styled as a job-platform row, search result, or navigation control.

No editorial influence

Advertisers do not influence which job platforms are listed, how they're categorized, or how search links are built. The list of job platforms reflects an editorial judgment about what's useful for job seekers in the UAE, independent of any advertising relationship.

Google AdSense and consent

Google AdSense is not currently enabled on the site. Before any Google-served advertising is introduced, the operator will review the placement, complete applicable account-side consent configuration, and update the Privacy Policy and this disclosure to describe the live setup accurately.

Affiliate or referral links

At present, the job-board links on this site are not affiliate or referral links, and we do not receive a commission when you click them or apply through them. If that changes for any platform in the future, we'll update this section to disclose it clearly, naming the specific platform and the nature of the relationship.

Questions

If you're unsure whether something on this site is an ad, a sponsored link, or an unpaid editorial link, feel free to ask — see the Contact page.

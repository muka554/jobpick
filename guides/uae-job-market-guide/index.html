A focused first week

Create an evidence-led job-search system.

Job boards are more useful when every application has a defined target, an original source, and a follow-up record. Start with this sequence before increasing the number of applications.

1

Define the target

Write two or three role titles, your core skills, preferred emirates or cities, availability, and the type of work arrangement you can realistically accept.

2

Prepare proof

Keep a role-specific CV, a clean portfolio or LinkedIn link where relevant, and accurate evidence for qualifications, employment dates, and notice period.

3

Use several routes

Combine employer career pages and selected job platforms with the relevant official government portal. Each route has different eligibility and vacancy coverage.

4

Track and verify

Record the role, employer, URL, date, submitted CV version, and response. Verify each employer independently before sending sensitive documents or money.

Public-sector map

Official UAE government career routes.

The UAE Government directs job seekers to federal and emirate-level public portals. These are distinct from commercial job boards, and each portal can set its own eligibility, account, document, and vacancy requirements.[1]

Use government portals purposefully.

Register only when a vacancy and your eligibility make the route relevant. Keep separate notes for portal account details, documents requested, and application reference numbers. A public portal is not a guarantee of eligibility or an offer.

Continue with private-sector routes.

For commercial roles, start with JobPick’s UAE platform directory, then look for the employer’s own careers page. Compare role details against the original employer source before treating a listing as current.

Market preparation

Set expectations before you apply.

This page does not publish salary figures without a transparent, current methodology. Instead, use a consistent comparison sheet so that your search decisions are based on the actual terms of a role rather than a headline number.

Role and levelCompare the exact title, seniority, core responsibilities, and any licensure requirement; similarly named roles can have very different scope.
Total packageAsk what is included in the stated compensation and whether any benefits, insurance, transport, housing, or bonus terms are contingent or separate.
Location and practical costRecord expected work location, commute, remote or hybrid pattern, and any relocation requirements before making comparisons.
Availability and permissionState only accurate residence status and notice period. Confirm changing visa, work-permit, and licensing requirements through the relevant official authority.

Visa and work permission: keep the source close.

The UAE Government describes a jobseeker visit visa with 60-, 90-, and 120-day options for qualifying applicants, along with specific eligibility and document requirements.[2] Searching for work and beginning work are separate steps; use the official service page to check the current rules that apply to you.

Employment formalities are employer-led.

The UAE Government says MoHRE issues work permits to registered establishments according to the role and circumstances, and says work requires a valid permit.[3] Do not rely on a guide, message, or recruiter to determine your own legal position.

Application safety

Make verification part of every application.

A professional-looking message is not proof of a live role or a legitimate employer. Take a short pause before submitting sensitive information or accepting an offer.

Verify the employer independently.

Find the organisation’s official website and careers page yourself. Compare the domain, recruiter name, vacancy title, location, and contact method rather than relying only on a forwarded message or social-media profile.

Original-source check

Keep personal data proportionate.

Use a role-specific CV and professional contact details. Do not send banking credentials, account passwords, or payment information as part of a routine job application. Escalate unusual document requests with care.

Data minimisation

Compare offer terms in writing.

Save the job description and written offer. MOHRE provides a service to view the details of an approved contract; use official channels for current contract procedures and questions.[4]

Gulf Job Websites List: Boards and Platforms Explained

By Ahmed Abayzeed · Middle East Job Hub · Last updated: August 25, 2026

Editorially reviewed for clarity, source context, and internal links on August 25, 2026.

This practical Gulf job websites list groups the main job boards, recruitment platforms, employer career pages, classified listings, and public employment portals used across the region. It explains what each type is for and when it is worth your time.

1General & Global Boards

Think LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor — platforms that cover every industry and every country, including the Gulf. Their strength is sheer volume and strong company/reviews data; their weakness is that Gulf-specific roles sometimes get buried among global listings. Good starting point, but don't stop here.

2Gulf Specialist Boards

Platforms like Bayt and GulfTalent that focus specifically on MENA and Gulf hiring. Employers who want candidates already based in or familiar with the region often post here first, sometimes exclusively. If you're serious about a Gulf move, these deserve equal attention to the global boards, not an afterthought.

3Recruitment Agencies

Agencies act as intermediaries — companies pay them to fill roles, so registering doesn't cost you anything as a candidate (see our scam warning guide if anyone asks otherwise). They're especially useful for mid-to-senior and specialized roles that companies prefer to fill discreetly rather than through public postings. Submitting your CV to a few reputable agencies in your field can surface roles you'd never find by searching.

4Classifieds

General classifieds sites carry job listings alongside everything else being bought, sold, or advertised locally. Quality varies more here than on dedicated job boards, but they're useful for smaller local businesses, retail, and hospitality roles that don't always bother posting on formal job boards.

5Government & National Platforms

Programmes tied to national employment initiatives — Emiratisation schemes in the UAE, or national workforce platforms in Saudi Arabia. These typically require you to register directly on the portal rather than searching from outside, and they're most relevant if you're a national of that country or targeting roles specifically tied to those programmes.

Putting it together

A thorough search touches at least the first three categories. Most people default to general boards alone and wonder why results feel thin — the Gulf specialists and agencies are often where the better-matched roles actually are.

Application evidence checklist

This page distinguishes platform categories rather than ranking individual employers. Re-check an external platform’s own help and privacy pages before creating an account or uploading a CV.

  • Save the original job URL, employer name, date seen, and application channel in a simple log.
  • Use a role-specific CV and keep copies of the version you submit; do not add qualifications, salary history, or availability claims you cannot support.
  • Confirm interview invitations through the employer’s official careers page or independently found contact details, not only through a message or forwarded link.
  • Do not pay a recruitment fee or share identity, banking, or account credentials until the employer and hiring stage are independently verified.

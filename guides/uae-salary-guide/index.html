Source-led pay comparison

UAE salary guide: compare the role, not just the number

Published salary estimates for eight role families, with the title, location and evidence behind each range. Use them to ask better questions about an offer, not as a promise of what you will earn.

By Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team · Published and reviewed 21 November 2026

No single range describes a whole profession. The figures below are third-party estimates, not JobPick payroll data, verified offers, legal minimums or guaranteed pay. Recruiter placement ranges and jobseeker-reported ranges measure different populations. We keep them separate rather than combine them into an artificial market average.

Start with the evidence label

All amounts on this page are AED per month. A range is not a progression ladder: its low end is not automatically a graduate salary, and its high end is not a reward that arrives after a fixed number of years. Match the responsibilities, experience, location and package before comparing.

Recruiter guide: Charterhouse's 2026 guide uses its candidate database, client input and market research. Its PDF describes an analysis of UAE placements over the previous year with forecasts for 2026. Figures are gross monthly fixed compensation including basic pay, housing and transport; its notes exclude education, leave travel, insurance and other perks. Role-level sample sizes are not given. These estimates describe the recruiter's segment, not every employer or applicant.

Jobseeker reports: Naukrigulf identifies its figures as salary insights from jobseeker data. The pages show a reported range, average, sample count and broad experience span. The visible pages do not establish a consistent basic-versus-total-package definition or collection period. We cannot independently audit the reports. The range is neither a confidence interval nor the minimum and maximum that any employer may offer.

The 2026 label belongs to the recruiter guide's edition. The Naukrigulf figures are a snapshot checked on 21 November 2026, not a newly conducted 2026 JobPick survey. Follow the linked sources for later changes.

Why another site may show a different average

Indeed's UAE Customer Service Representative page shows an average base salary of AED 3,303 per month from 2.5k reports, with a stated update date of 11 May 2026. That differs from the Dubai-only Naukrigulf estimate above in geography, timing, sample and potentially pay definition. Neither number proves the other wrong. We do not average them together or use the gap as evidence that pay has risen or fallen.

What to check in each role family

IT and software

Use the software engineer figures only after matching the work: product development, business systems, testing and infrastructure are not interchangeable. Ask what you will build, the level of ownership, whether on-call work is involved and how seniority is defined. A recruiter's software-engineering range is not a UAE-wide floor, and it does not establish what someone without professional experience will receive. Prepare evidence with the IT application guide.

Technical support

Compare desktop support with the desktop-support row, not a support manager or software developer. Clarify whether the job covers first-line enquiries, field visits, systems administration or escalation ownership. Ask about shifts, call-outs, travel and how any additional payments are treated. The wide IT Support Engineer range is a reason to ask about scope, not to assume every candidate can negotiate its top end. See the technical-support guide.

Customer service

The reported customer-service range is for Dubai and combines a broad experience span. It cannot tell you the rate for a specific language, employer, industry or shift. Confirm whether the role is inbound service, complaints handling, collections or sales with targets. If a vacancy mixes service with selling, ask what is fixed pay and what is conditional. Use the customer-service guide to prepare relevant examples.

Sales

Separate guaranteed fixed pay from commission, target-linked bonus and any advertised on-target earnings. Ask for the written target, payout trigger, payment timing, treatment of cancellations and whether the figure assumes full target attainment. Do not add hoped-for commission to the fixed range when deciding whether your necessary expenses are covered. A sales manager's range also needs a comparable team, account or territory responsibility. See the sales application guide.

Finance and accounting

The gap between the Charterhouse General Accountant (Financial) estimate and Naukrigulf's Dubai Accountant aggregate is substantial. The titles, populations, locations and package definitions are not identical. We cannot explain the difference as a precise premium for one qualification or employer type. Match the actual duties: transaction processing, reconciliations, reporting, controls and team responsibility. Verify how any certification requirement is described in the vacancy. See the finance guide.

Logistics and operations

The coordinator and manager rows describe different jobs. Ask about responsibility for stock, shipments, suppliers, staff, budgets and operational exceptions before using either. Check shift patterns, site location, transport arrangements and any travel. A broad reported coordinator range is not evidence that a particular employer offers its maximum. Prepare a clear account of your own process and exception-handling work with the logistics guide.

Healthcare

The healthcare figure is a small, 29-report sample for Registered General Nurse, not a range for doctors, pharmacists, specialist nurses or the whole sector. It does not establish licensing eligibility, recognition of qualifications or permission to practise. Confirm the exact clinical or non-clinical role, employer, facility, supervision, shift pattern and the relevant regulatory route independently. The sample does not justify a rate for a newly qualified applicant. Start with the boundaries in the healthcare guide.

Hospitality

The Dubai Guest Service Agent figure cannot stand in for chefs, restaurant supervisors, housekeeping managers or hotel management. Ask what the cash salary includes and whether accommodation, meals, transport, service charge or tips are separate. Confirm which amounts are guaranteed in writing and which vary; do not treat an estimate of tips as fixed income. Check the employing entity, department and shift requirements using the hospitality guide.

Compare the actual package, line by line

Before accepting or rejecting an offer on a headline number, ask for a written breakdown. The UAE Government's job-offer guidance explains the offer and contract process and identifies salary among contract terms. Use the official process relevant to your employer; this page is not legal advice.

  • Fixed cash: basic salary and each fixed allowance. Ask whether housing and transport are included in the quoted number or paid separately. Never add an allowance twice.
  • Conditional pay: commission, bonus, overtime, service charge and tips. Record the conditions and exclude uncertain amounts from your guaranteed-cash comparison.
  • Non-cash arrangements: accommodation, transport, meals and insurance. Ask what is actually provided and any limits. Do not assign a made-up cash value to a benefit you have not inspected.
  • Time and place: hours, shifts, work location, travel and commuting costs. Two equal monthly figures may leave you with different time and essential expenses.
  • Personal decision: compare verified guaranteed cash with your own realistic budget. None of these survey figures defines a salary that is automatically enough for every household.

Make a small comparison sheet for each real offer: employer and title; written basic pay; fixed allowances; guaranteed monthly cash; conditional amounts; non-cash benefits; schedule; location; unclear terms to confirm. Keep the source document and the date beside each number. Do not let a calculator turn an unconfirmed allowance into a fact.

Discuss salary without false precision

A useful opening is: "Could you share the budgeted fixed monthly package for this role, and whether housing and transport are included?" Once the responsibilities are clear, explain which comparable source you used and why the job matches it. Do not claim a salary report guarantees your personal value or that every employer must match it.

If the employer's number is outside a source range, ask about the difference in duties, seniority or package. That gap alone does not prove an offer is unlawful or fraudulent, and a number inside a range does not prove an employer is legitimate. Use our employer-verification guide separately. Never invent another offer or past salary to support a negotiation.

Where the evidence is weak, say so. We have not verified a current pay range for every speciality, every emirate or every level of experience. We have not audited an employer payroll, assessed any candidate's eligibility or made a cost-of-living promise. These estimates are a starting point for questions, not a replacement for a real written offer.

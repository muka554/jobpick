Amman city guide

Finding jobs in Amman

By Middle East Job Hub Editorial Team · Last reviewed: August 26, 2026 · Informational guidance

What changed: expanded local search routes, practical city-search planning, and source notes reviewed on August 26, 2026.

An Amman search becomes more efficient when it uses the national employment and guidance routes for planning, then applies through a verified employer or public source with a role-specific evidence file.

Use Sajjil as a verified public route

Jordan’s Ministry of Labour says its Employment Directorate provides direct employment through the National Employment Platform, Sajjil, alongside career guidance, counseling, CV and interview support, and employment services. [1] The Sajjil platform itself describes job and training search for individuals. [3]

Use those public routes to understand the process and current opportunities. Do not treat a platform listing as a hiring guarantee, and do not assume a national route overrides an employer’s own vacancy requirements.

Build role clusters from your evidence

For local planning, group applications by the work you can demonstrate: technology and customer support, finance and accounting, education and student services, sales and account management, operations, manufacturing support, design, HR, or project coordination. Search with exact terms such as “software support”, “accounts officer”, “customer experience”, “learning coordinator”, “production planner”, or “business development executive”.

These are editorial search prompts, not labour-market forecasts or claims about an active employer. The value comes from matching the title to documented skills, work samples, and responsibilities.

Make location and working hours explicit

Before prioritizing a role, log the stated worksite, hours, travel expectation, client visits, and on-site or remote wording. If a post is vague, ask a verified employer or recruiter at the appropriate stage. This is an editorial application-planning check, not transport or employment advice.

Use guidance and employer research together

The Ministry’s public material includes employment guidance, an employment services guide, a private employment-office directory, and labour-market resources. [2] Use them to establish a reliable source trail. Then check the employer’s own site, profile, and official contact channels before you send documents or follow up.

How to use platforms safely and efficiently

Apply once through the source you can identify. Save the original job URL, the employer domain, date seen, contact route, and the CV version used. A request for a fee, payment information, a password, or documents before a transparent process is a reason to pause and independently verify the employer.

Sources and official routes

These sources support the public-route statements above. They do not confirm an individual vacancy, eligibility decision, or hiring outcome.

  1. Jordan Ministry of Labour — Employment Directorate documents Sajjil, direct employment, career guidance, job fairs, and employment services.
  2. Jordan Ministry of Labour — Employment services lists guidance, employment-service, private-office, and labour-market resources.
  3. Sajjil — National Employment Platform is the official public platform for job and training search.
Editorial note: The local planning observations in this guide are editorial judgment, not a forecast of hiring or a statement of eligibility. Confirm the worksite, working arrangement, application requirements, and current source conditions directly with the employer or public authority.

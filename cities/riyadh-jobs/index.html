Find Jobs in Riyadh

By Ahmed Abayzeed · Middle East Job Hub · Last updated: August 25, 2026

Editorially reviewed for clarity, source context, and internal links on August 25, 2026.

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia's capital, has seen accelerated hiring growth driven by Vision 2030 and the giga-projects program, with many international and regional companies establishing or expanding their headquarters there.

Key sectors

  • Government & PIF-linked entities — the Public Investment Fund and its portfolio companies are major employers across finance, infrastructure, and giga-project development
  • Regional headquarters — Saudi Arabia's push to require regional HQs for companies doing significant government business has brought many multinational corporate offices to Riyadh specifically
  • Consulting & finance — a fast-growing sector supporting the pace of large-scale national projects
  • Construction & engineering — driven directly by giga-project activity and continued infrastructure expansion
  • Technology — an increasingly active startup and enterprise tech scene, supported by national digital transformation initiatives

Saudization considerations

Saudi Arabia's Nitaqat (Saudization) programme sets quotas for Saudi national employment by company and sector, which affects how readily companies can sponsor expat hires depending on their current quota status. This doesn't mean expat roles aren't available — they very much are, especially in specialized and senior positions — but it's worth understanding as context for how hiring timelines and role availability can vary by employer. National platforms like those listed in our Government & National Platforms section (Nafis, Taqat) are primarily geared toward Saudi nationals but are useful for understanding the broader employment landscape.

Tips for searching Riyadh specifically

  • Search for giga-project names directly alongside your role — much of the highest-profile hiring is tied to specific named projects rather than generic "Saudi Arabia" postings
  • If you're in consulting, finance, or engineering, watch for regional headquarters announcements from multinational firms — these often bring a wave of hiring as offices scale up
  • Riyadh's formal, government-adjacent hiring processes can take longer than private-sector timelines elsewhere in the Gulf — plan accordingly

Official resource and verification step

Jadarat is an official service for job seekers to browse and apply for available roles. The official page explains its profile, search, and application steps and identifies its intended audience.

Primary reference reviewed August 25, 2026: GOV.SA — Apply for jobs on the Jadarat platform.

Application evidence checklist

For Riyadh searches, separate employer careers pages from national-platform routes. Check the official service’s current audience and access conditions before treating a role as generally available.

  • Save the original job URL, employer name, date seen, and application channel in a simple log.
  • Use a role-specific CV and keep copies of the version you submit; do not add qualifications, salary history, or availability claims you cannot support.
  • Confirm interview invitations through the employer’s official careers page or independently found contact details, not only through a message or forwarded link.
  • Do not pay a recruitment fee or share identity, banking, or account credentials until the employer and hiring stage are independently verified.

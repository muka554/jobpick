Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This policy describes the current privacy practices of Middle East Job Hub at jobpick20.com. It applies to the public site and should be read together with the privacy notice of any third-party platform you visit through a link here.

1. What this site is

Middle East Job Hub ("we," "us," "the site") is a job-search link aggregator. It does not require an account, does not collect job applications, and does not process payments.

2. Information we collect

We do not operate a server-side backend and do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number through this site. The search box on the homepage runs entirely in your browser — what you type is used only to build search links to third-party job platforms and is never sent to us or stored by us. If you use the optional Recent searches feature, up to six role, country, and city combinations are stored only in your browser so you can re-run them; you can clear them at any time with the Clear history button or by clearing browser storage.

3. Privacy choices, cookies, and advertising

We show a privacy-choice notice before loading optional analytics. You can choose “Essential only” to use the site without optional analytics, or choose “Accept optional analytics” to allow Google Analytics. Your selection is stored in your browser so we can remember it; you can change it at any time using the “Privacy choices” button.

The interactive job-search hub does not currently display Google AdSense units. Before any advertising is enabled, we will review the placement, configure the applicable account-side privacy settings, and use a Google-certified consent-management solution where Google requires one for the audience location. You can manage Google advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings and read Google’s advertising technology information.

Visitors in some regions may have additional rights and consent requirements. This site-level privacy notice does not replace any consent configuration that must be completed in an advertising account before advertising is enabled.

4. Third-party links

Almost everything on this site is a link to a third-party job platform, recruitment agency, or government portal. Once you leave this site, that platform's own privacy policy applies — we have no control over and no responsibility for how they handle your data. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link here.

5. Analytics

Only after you choose to allow optional analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand high-level traffic patterns such as page views, general device information, referral sources, and aggregated interactions with site features. These interactions can include a search submission, use of a saved search, an external-platform link click, a guide call-to-action click, or use of the printable checklist. We do not send the words typed into the search box, job applications, payment information, or account registration details to analytics. The search box builds external search links in your browser; we do not operate a server-side job-application database.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you (for example under GDPR or similar regional laws). Since we don't collect or store personal data directly, most such requests should be directed to the third-party platforms you interacted with. For anything related to this site directly, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Contact page, or email wiz.mkawe@gmail.com directly.

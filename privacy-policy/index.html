Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This policy describes the current privacy practices of Middle East Job Hub at jobpick20.com. It applies to the public site and should be read together with the privacy notice of any third-party platform you visit through a link here.

1. What this site is

Middle East Job Hub ("we," "us," "the site") is the publisher of jobpick20.com. JobPick is the name used for the site’s interactive job-search experience. The public directory does not require an account, does not collect job applications, and does not process payments.

2. Browser-local search and application tools

The homepage search box uses the role, country, and city you choose to build links to third-party job platforms in your browser. By default, the six most recent role, country, and city combinations are stored only in your browser so you can run them again. The optional Private Application Tracker is also browser-local: it can store up to 50 company, role, status, and date entries on your device. It does not sync through the account feature. You can clear either tool with its on-page clear action or by clearing browser storage.

3. Optional account and synchronized search history

If you choose to use optional account features, you may sign in with Google, a password, or an email Magic Link. Authentication is provided through Supabase. For signed-in visitors who choose to synchronize Recent searches, the role text (up to 80 characters), country, city, and record timestamps are sent to and stored in the Supabase project used by the site so those search-history entries can be merged across devices. The feature keeps up to six active synchronized entries. It is optional and is not needed to browse the directory.

Selecting Clear history removes browser-local history and marks your active synchronized history as deleted from ordinary retrieval. The site does not synchronize Private Application Tracker entries. To ask about a synchronized-history record or an account, contact us using the details below.

4. Location tools

The “Use my location” tool uses your browser’s location permission to choose a supported country and, where available, a nearby supported city. The “Use approximate location” tool makes one request only when you click it to an IP-based geolocation provider, limited to country code and city fields. Your public IP address is processed by that provider as part of the request; JobPick uses the returned country and recognized city only to update the page and does not store those results or send them to site analytics. You can instead choose country and city manually.

5. Privacy choices, cookies, and advertising

We show a privacy-choice notice before loading optional analytics. You can choose “Essential only” to use the site without optional analytics, or choose “Accept optional analytics” to allow Google Analytics. Your selection is stored in your browser so we can remember it; you can change it at any time using the “Privacy choices” button.

The interactive job-search hub does not currently display Google AdSense units. Before any advertising is enabled, we will review the placement, configure the applicable account-side privacy settings, and use a Google-certified consent-management solution where Google requires one for the audience location. You can manage Google advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings and read Google’s advertising technology information.

Visitors in some regions may have additional rights and consent requirements. This site-level privacy notice does not replace any consent configuration that must be completed in an advertising account before advertising is enabled.

6. Third-party links

Almost everything on this site is a link to a third-party job platform, recruitment agency, government portal, or information provider. Once you leave this site, that provider's own privacy policy applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link here.

7. Analytics

Only after you choose to allow optional analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand high-level traffic patterns such as page views, general device information, referral sources, and aggregated interactions with site features. These interactions can include a search submission, use of a saved search, an external-platform link click, a guide call-to-action click, or use of the printable checklist. We do not send the words typed into the search box, job applications, payment information, precise device location, IP-location response, or account registration details to analytics. The search box builds external search links in your browser; we do not operate a server-side job-application database.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you (for example under GDPR or similar regional laws). Requests concerning third-party job platforms or the approximate-location provider should be directed to the relevant provider. For browser-local data, you can use the clear actions described above. For optional account or synchronized-history questions, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Contact page, or email wiz.mkawe@gmail.com directly.

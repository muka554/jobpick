Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against your actual setup (domain, entity, jurisdiction, and any tools you add later) before publishing, ideally by someone qualified where you're based.

1. What this site is

Gulf Job Hub ("we," "us," "the site") is a job-search link aggregator. It does not require an account, does not collect job applications, and does not process payments.

2. Information we collect

We do not operate a server-side backend and do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number through this site. The search box on the homepage runs entirely in your browser — what you type is used only to build search links to third-party job platforms and is never sent to us or stored by us.

3. Cookies and advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.
  • Google's own use of advertising cookies is governed by its Advertising Policies.

Some rows on this site link to sponsored or affiliate offers, clearly marked "Advertisement" — see our Advertising Disclosure for details.

4. Third-party links

Almost everything on this site is a link to a third-party job platform, recruitment agency, or government portal. Once you leave this site, that platform's own privacy policy applies — we have no control over and no responsibility for how they handle your data. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link here.

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand overall traffic to this site — for example, approximate page views, general geographic region, device type, and referral source. This does not include your name, email, or any information you didn't already choose to give us. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this; see Google's own Privacy Policy for how it handles that data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using a browser/extension that blocks analytics scripts.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you (for example under GDPR or similar regional laws). Since we don't collect or store personal data directly, most such requests should be directed to the third-party platforms you interacted with. For anything related to this site directly, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Contact page, or email wiz.mkawe@gmail.com directly.

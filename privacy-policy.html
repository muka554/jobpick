Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Gulf Job Hub (“we,” “us,” or “the Site”) is a job-search link aggregator for the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. The Site does not require an account, host job listings, accept applications, or process payments.

Information you enter

The search box operates in your browser. Search terms and selections are used to build links to third-party job platforms and are not submitted to or stored on our server. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in that email and use it to respond to your request.

Cookies and advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled on the Site, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, measure, and limit advertisements. Their use of information is governed by Google’s policies and your available privacy controls. We do not control third-party advertising systems.

We do not currently use a separate analytics service on this static website. If that changes, this policy will be updated to describe the service, information collected, and available choices before or when the service is introduced.

Third-party websites

Most links lead to external job platforms, recruitment agencies, or government portals. Once you leave the Site, the destination’s privacy policy and terms apply. Review them before creating an account or providing personal information.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and questions

Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights under applicable law. Because the Site does not maintain user accounts or a personal-data database, requests concerning information held by an external platform should be directed to that platform. For questions about this Site, email wiz.mkawe@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the Site’s features or legal requirements change. The updated date above indicates the latest revision.

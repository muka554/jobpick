# Support and customer-service workshop sources

Checked 22 November 2026. Release 3 expands two existing English role guides, not new indexable pages. The linked Arabic pages remain core overviews rather than a claimed translation of these exercises.

## Original teaching material

Both packs, their people, applications, order/case/ticket references, times, instructions and outcomes are invented. No incident, customer, company, refund, tenant, log query, account action or interview was involved. The support case remains unresolved. The complaint remains open, with no refund approval or payment. No real user messages or identifiers are published.

Technical support asks the reader to separate observations from hypotheses, respect authorisation, check scope and business impact, and draft a handover with unaccepted transfer and continuing update ownership. The successful sign-in and HTTP 403 are supplied fictional observations, not a Microsoft test or diagnosis. Customer service uses a supplied complaint, incomplete history, delivery status and logged review requests. It separates recording a request from approval/payment and does not invent a delivery date or legal remedy.

The 09:30/17:00 updates, permissions, 100-word reply limit and training policy are JobPick's exercise choices, not universal SLAs or an employer's terms. Self-review checklists are not hiring scores. The customer worked reply has 87 whitespace-separated words. No script, email, account action or monitoring commitment is created by publishing a sample.

## Current source ledger

1. Microsoft Learn, How to troubleshoot sign-in errors, Microsoft Entra ID. Primary product documentation, no publication date relied on.
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/monitoring-health/howto-troubleshoot-sign-in-errors
   Supports collecting appropriate sign-in diagnostic details, including correlation information, within appropriate access. The source names prerequisites and a Reports Reader role for activity logs. Our page does not grant the reader that role or claim every first-line analyst has access. It does not establish the cause of a fictional application-side 403 after a successful sign-in. No tenant was opened and no log access was tested.

2. MDN, HTTP 403 Forbidden. Technical reference/standards explanation, no update date relied on.
   https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Status/403
   Supports the meaning that the server understood the request but refuses it. We do not infer a particular organisation's role mapping, missing group, broken password, compromised account or safe fix from the status alone. No live HTTP failure was induced.

3. Atlassian, Understanding incident severity levels. Primary vendor process guidance, no update date relied on.
   https://www.atlassian.com/incident-management/kpis/severity-levels
   Supports distinguishing impact/severity and urgency/priority and adapting definitions to the organisation. Its example SEV model is not imposed on a reader. One reporting user does not automatically justify a low-priority label; actual business impact, scope and approved workaround remain relevant unknowns.

4. Atlassian, Incident communication best practices. Primary vendor process guidance; fetched result gives a 10 December 2025 publication date, not used as a freshness guarantee.
   https://www.atlassian.com/incident-management/incident-communication
   Supports communicating known impact, audience-appropriate information and the next update. The page's specific recommended cadence is not made into a universal SLA. Our fictional times and training ownership instructions are editorial, not Atlassian's or an employer's acceptance of an obligation.

5. Public Service Ombudsman, Principles of good complaint handling, Summary. Public-body complaint-handling guidance, no date relied on.
   https://www.ombudsman.org.uk/about-us/our-principles/principles-good-complaint-handling/summary
   Supports listening, honest evidence-based explanations, accurate records and proportionate handling. Used as a communication reference only. These UK public-body principles do not determine UAE retail rights, a refund amount, return timing, a retailer's policy or the outcome of our invented complaint. The guide explicitly says not to use the training policy to deny a legal right.

6. National Careers Service, The STAR method. Government careers advice, no date relied on.
   https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method
   Supports situation/task/action/result, truthful personally relevant examples, short explanations and follow-up preparation. The new role-specific questions and examples are JobPick editorial practice, not employer questions or proof of interview frequency. A fictional exercise must not become a claim of real paid experience.

7. National Careers Service, Interview tips. Government careers advice, no date relied on.
   https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice
   Supports reading the vacancy, employer research, practice and following assessment instructions. It does not establish that all Dubai employers conduct a role-play, switch languages without warning or use our checklists. Such older assertions were removed.

8. UAE Government, Searching for a job. Official UAE job-search guidance, no date relied on.
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/find-a-job/searching-for-a-job
   Supports checking routes and the statement that jobseekers do not pay recruitment-agency fees. An optional course unrelated to securing a job is not automatically treated as a recruitment fee, and paying for training does not verify a vacancy. No particular recruiter, vacancy or eligibility was investigated.

## Removed or narrowed statements

- Unsupported assertions about sector concentration, most-copied listings, service-job scam frequency, typical hiring behaviour and mandatory surprise language assessment.
- Unlabelled impressive numerical CV examples: technical ticket/user volumes and an asserted knowledge-base result; service call volume and quality/resolution claims. Neither was a documented candidate result. New CV lines describe only a labelled fictional exercise, with no invented production metrics.
- Universal assumptions about shift patterns, client access or clearance. Search lanes are editorial prompts; readers confirm duties, permissions, site and language requirements with the actual vacancy.
- Broad recruitment-cost wording now uses the specific official agency-fee guidance and keeps an unrelated optional course distinct.

# Platform walkthroughs: source and editorial evidence, 22 November 2026

## Purpose and scope

Enrich the existing UAE employment-route comparison, not create another indexable page. Reader question: what can I inspect, and what account/data decision appears next, before I register? Audience: jobseekers comparing routes. The six existing examples remain FAHR, Dubai Careers, Bayt, Hays, DHL and Accor. These are not ranked providers, verified employers or vacancy recommendations.

Research combined official UAE guidance, operator descriptions, and direct public browser interactions. Independent review or community opinion would not establish the form actually reached, so neither substitutes for these primary observations. All journeys took place on 22 November 2026, about 12:43-12:51 Dubai time, without candidate data, registration, login, uploaded files, payment or final submission. Public keyword/location searches and role-specific apply navigation were the only form-like actions. Existing signed-in candidate state was not used.

## 1. UAE Government job-search guidance

- URL: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/find-a-job/searching-for-a-job
- Publisher: UAE Government portal. Type: official public guidance. Displayed update: 10 November 2026. Checked 22 November 2026.
- Supports channel categories, federal and Dubai portal identification, free portal registration with possible optional paid extras, no commission for obtaining a job through a portal, and employer-borne recruitment-agency fees.
- Does not verify any sampled advertiser, eligibility, checkout, paid product or individual vacancy. No UAE rule was transferred to all Gulf countries. No independent legal conclusion was added.
- Confidence: high for the page's stated guidance; not an operator-by-operator payment audit.

## 2. FAHR and federal iRecruitment

- Official entry: https://www.fahr.gov.ae/en/about-us/jobs/
- Stable portal linked by FAHR: https://www.federalerp.gov.ae/OA_HTML/IrcVisitor.jsp?menu=Y
- Publisher: FAHR/federal recruitment portal. Types: official gateway description and directly observed visitor interface. Checked about 12:44 Dubai time.
- FAHR invites registration. The linked portal also exposed a public visitor keyword search, entity and date controls, Register today and Login here. Entering support with no entity/date selection returned Available Jobs with no results.
- Correction: the older guide's wording based only on the gateway could imply registration was necessary even to inspect a search. Our public visitor journey disproves that narrower implication.
- Limits: one exact query, not evidence that federal vacancies do not exist, that all public search queries work, that a suitable detail is reachable or that an application is possible without an account. No registration or detail/application tested. Temporary portal session URLs deliberately excluded from the article.
- Evidence: public gateway/visitor/search browser captures and screenshot. Confidence: high for the recorded session only.

## 3. Dubai Careers

- Entry: https://dubaicareers.ae/en/Pages/default.aspx
- Public search: https://jobs.dubaicareers.ae/careersection/dubaicareers/jobsearch.ftl?lang=en
- Sample: https://jobs.dubaicareers.ae/careersection/dubaicareers/jobdetail.ftl?job=26002008&tz=GMT%2B04%3A00&tzname=Asia%2FDubai
- Publisher: Dubai Careers. Types: official public homepage, search/detail, login boundary. Checked about 12:48-12:49 Dubai time.
- All Jobs followed by View All Jobs produced a loaded list. The homepage offered candidate/category routes including UAE Nationals, Fresh Graduates and a Private Sector Jobs link. The whole portal was not reduced to one universal nationality or employer category.
- Sample Senior Internal Auditor 26002008 exposed Required Nationality: UAE Only and other conditions publicly. Apply Online reached Login with existing-user and New User choices.
- Limits: only that advert's nationality restriction; not every Dubai Careers role. Initial asynchronously empty list was not used as evidence. No login, Google sign-in, account creation or application beyond login.
- Confidence: high for this observed public route; eligibility remains vacancy-specific.

## 4. Bayt

- Entry: https://www.bayt.com/en/uae/
- Public Dubai list: https://www.bayt.com/en/uae/jobs/jobs-in-dubai/
- Sample: https://www.bayt.com/en/uae/jobs/hr-manager-5512362/
- The public list exposed the same sample in a detail drawer with jobId=5512362. Its Easy Apply led to Create account.
- Publisher: Bayt. Types: operator public discovery/detail and initial registration interface, with direct DOM check of current checkbox state. Checked about 12:50-12:51 Dubai time.
- UAE landing-page country control displayed All locations in our session. Used the visible jobs in dubai route instead of assuming the landing URL enforced location.
- HR Manager 5512362 attributed the advert to Jigsaw HR Consultants. This is page attribution, not verified identity, ownership, licence or hiring authority.
- Registration showed name, email, password and mobile fields, existing-user and alternative identity options. Public-profile visibility, marketing-email and WhatsApp choices were checked by default. No values or defaults changed.
- The visibility label included a promotional uplift percentage. It is excluded from the article as unsupported outcome evidence. Vacancy totals and testimonials are also excluded. No claim about exact data exposed, deletion, retention, paid products or every listing's flow.
- Evidence: public pages, registration screenshot, explicit DOM checkbox-state capture. Tracking/session additions to destination URLs excluded. Confidence: high for these session defaults, not a platform-wide permanence claim.

## 5. Hays UAE

- Generic CV route, supporting context only: https://www.hays.ae/account/send-cv
- Sample: https://www.hays.ae/job-detail/sales-support-manager-abu-dhabi_1027803?jobSource=HaysGCJ

- Publisher: Hays UAE. Types: operator process description and directly observed role/application form. Checked about 12:48-12:49 Dubai time.
- Sales Support Manager, Abu Dhabi, reference 1027803, described an unnamed client. The apply control offered Hays and LinkedIn paths; selected Hays only.
- Public application form asked name, email and CV without a prior sign-in. It explicitly said applying would create a My Hays account and email a password-setting link. Displayed .doc/.docx/.rtf/.txt/.pdf and 3MB maximum; covering letter requested within the CV file if supplied.
- General FAQ consultant-contact description is not a claim that every current advert has identical mechanics. Generic send-CV is not an application to this role.
- Limits: no candidate data, upload, checkbox change, Apply, account creation, representation, consultant/client verification or confirmation tested. No account-free submission claim. Confidence: high for explicit sampled-form notice.

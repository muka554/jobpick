# Saudi job-search guide — source notes

**Research date:** 27 August 2026

These notes support a country-level Saudi job-search guide. They are not individual employment, immigration, legal, or eligibility advice. Source pages and service conditions may change; each link must be rechecked during future editorial reviews.

| Intended guide use | Primary source | Verified finding | Editorial boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official Saudi job-search route for Saudi citizens | [HRDF: Jadarat — Individuals](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/en/products-and-services/programs/individuals/other/jadarat/) | HRDF describes Jadarat as a national employment platform for Saudi workforce employment in public and private sectors. Its page states that applicants for the service must be Saudi nationals and that advertised jobs are for Saudi citizens. | Do not describe Jadarat as the general route for non-Saudi applicants. |
| Jadarat process | [GOV.SA: Apply for jobs on Jadarat](https://my.gov.sa/en/services/19019) | The national portal lists Nafath login, a professional profile, job search, and applications as steps. It lists Arabic and English as service languages. | Present as the official service’s published process, not a guarantee of access or a job. |
| Offer and employment-contract context | [MHRSD: Labour contracts](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/knowledge-centre/articles/64399) | MHRSD defines an employment contract and identifies core contract information, including employer and worker identity, remuneration, type/place of work, joining date, duration where limited, rights, and obligations. | Encourage readers to read the actual contract and confirm current rules; do not interpret a particular contract. |
| Expatriate work-formality context | [MHRSD: Issue and renew work license](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%88-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%B1%D8%AE%D8%B5-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84) | The Ministry describes an establishment-facing service to issue or renew work licences for expatriate workers and names Qiwa as the service channel. | Distinguish an employer’s formality from a candidate’s job-search step. Avoid legal/visa advice. |
| Existing resident’s post-offer process | [GOV.SA: Transfer of services from another employer](https://my.gov.sa/en/services/2792867) | GOV.SA describes a process in which the new employer submits a job offer on Qiwa, the worker accepts or rejects, and the system verifies conditions before issuance/update steps. | Apply only to the official-service context and direct readers to the service for current conditions. |
| Reporting potential private-sector labour violations | [MHRSD: Reporting violations of labour regulations](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%BA%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84) | MHRSD states that the service accepts reports of private-sector labour-regulation violations and routes them to the responsible Ministry team. | Do not characterize every suspicious recruitment interaction as a labour violation; state the link is an official reporting channel for a reader to assess. |

## Guide differentiation

The Saudi guide should answer a country-level planning question: **“Which official route fits my status, and what should I verify before and after a Saudi job application?”** This differentiates it from the existing Riyadh city page, which is location-focused, and from generic platform directory pages.

## Dubai and Abu Dhabi — UAE

| Source | URL | Supported use in city guides |
|---|---|---|
| UAE Government — Government jobs | https://u.ae/en/resources/government-jobs | The official page lists federal, Abu Dhabi Government, and Dubai Government job portals. Use it to distinguish official public-sector routes from general external platforms. |

| Dubai Careers | https://dubaicareers.ae/en/pages/default.aspx | The public Dubai Government platform exposes job categories and routes for UAE Nationals, fresh graduates, private-sector jobs, experienced applicants, and people of determination. |

Use it to explain why travel time and a location check matter for city-based applications; do not infer an active vacancy. |

The notes are source summaries, not legal, eligibility, or vacancy advice. All claims used in public guide copy must remain within the supported scope above.

# CV application workshop source ledger

Checked 22 November 2026. Release 2 expands the existing English CV toolkit in place, following ADSENSE_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md. No new indexable guide or keyword variant is added.

## Scope and original work

The practice vacancy, candidate Mira Nasser, both fictional organisations, diploma, dates, twelve-item example log, CV, supporting statement, message and workbook are invented editorial teaching material. No private candidate records, actual employer brief, real application, recruiter feedback, parsing result, hiring result or personal legal assessment is represented. Example details must not be copied as an applicant's own facts. No actual person is the subject of this workshop.

The learning path is vacancy requirements to evidence labels, a full CV, editing boundaries, file checks, application writing and a private final record. The printable sample repeats the same HTML CV. The blank three-page PDF repeats the HTML workbook prompts, with space for handwriting. The source is scripts/build-cv-workshop-print.py, which reads the guide and makes no network requests. This is not a fillable PDF or a new upload service. Existing AI-workspace disclosures and basic-checklist limitations remain.

## Current sources

1. National Careers Service, How to write a CV. Government career-advice source; no publication date shown in fetched content.
   https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections/
   Supports clear readable sections, tailoring, truthful contact/history details and most recent work first. UK writing advice, not a UAE legal or hiring convention. Does not establish a universal two-page rule or an interview guarantee.

2. National Careers Service, Application form advice. Government career-advice source; no publication date shown.
   https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/application-forms
   Supports relevant experience, checking answers and saving a copy. The exercise's 120-word requirement is original fiction, not taken from a real application. Personal-information examples on this UK page are not presented as UAE data requirements.

3. National Careers Service, How to write a cover letter. Government career-advice source; no publication date shown.
   https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/covering-letter/
   Supports referencing the role, relevant factual evidence, recipient checking and keeping the application. The source recommends a cover letter with a CV; our editorial approach defers to the actual vacancy instructions and avoids duplicate email when a portal/form is required. We do not copy its generic salutations or assume a named contact exists.

4. Oxford University Careers Service, CVs. University careers guidance; no publication date shown.
   https://www.careers.ox.ac.uk/cvs
   Supports role-specific emphasis, clear structure and evidence-based descriptions. Advice varies with career stage and employer instructions. Its numerical examples do not supply any numbers for our candidate. It is not evidence about UAE-wide ATS use or applicant success.

5. Oxford University Careers Service, Demonstrate You Fit the Job Criteria. University careers guidance; no publication date shown.
   https://www.careers.ox.ac.uk/demonstrate-you-fit-the-job-criteria
   Supports criterion-to-example mapping and examples from employment, study and other activities. Our direct/transferable/not-demonstrated labels and practice vacancy are JobPick's own editorial method, not Oxford's rating system. No sample from Oxford is reproduced as a JobPick candidate history.

6. Greenhouse Support, Unsuccessful resume parse. Primary vendor/product documentation, updated 2 March 2026.
   https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse
   Supports the description of possible parsing problems with images, complex tables, columns and contact details in document headers, footers or text boxes. States resumes over 2.5 MB cannot be parsed. Also notes fictional data may affect parsing. This is Greenhouse-specific, not a claim that all ATS products reject the same files. Our fictional PDF has not been submitted to or tested by Greenhouse.

7. Greenhouse Support, Supported formats for resumes, cover letters and other candidate uploads. Primary vendor/product documentation, updated 19 August 2026.
   https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-formats-for-resumes-cover-letters-and-other-candidate-uploads
   Lists supported formats and a 100 MB candidate-upload limit. This is not the same as the parser's 2.5 MB limit. We retain both scopes together, without applying either limit to a different platform or claiming upload success means correctly parsed fields.

8. UAE Government, Searching for a job. Official UAE job-search guidance; no date relied on.
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/find-a-job/searching-for-a-job
   Supports honest CVs and safe routes, not a mandatory UAE format, nationality/photo requirement, work-permission conclusion or personal eligibility result.

## Limits and deliberate choices

- The fictional CV uses one column, but there is no ATS certification or promised pass rate.
- Twelve is explicitly the count in one fictional weekly log. It is not a daily volume, resolved-case total, improvement percentage or real achievement.
- ERP experience is absent. Excel evidence is not relabelled as ERP use.
- City is a fictional profile detail. Commute, permission to work, shifts and start date remain unknown rather than inferred.
- Phone omitted from the sample to avoid printing a real number. Example email is plain text, not an outreach recipient.
- No real attachment, recipient, submission, interview or result exists in the exercise. The completed record says drafted, not submitted.
- National Careers Service and university guidance provide writing principles, not UAE immigration or data-collection rules.
- The English workshop and PDFs do not pretend to be a simultaneous Arabic translation. The Arabic link is described as the core checklist.
- All pre-release HTML IDs, original font-link markup and non-JSON-LD script tags are preserved. Printable PDFs are new assets, not advertising or tracking changes.

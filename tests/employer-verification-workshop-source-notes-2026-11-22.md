# UAE employer-check workshop: source and verification notes

Checked 22 November 2026. Public-source review only. No account created, private record searched, application submitted, company investigated or individual offer validated. The three worked cases are original fictional exercises, not reader reports or completed investigations.

## Sources and claims

1. UAE Government, National Economic Register
   https://u.ae/information-and-services/business/important-digital-services/national-economic-register
   Official service-scope source, dated 27 October 2026 on the page. Supports economic-licence/activity/trade-name enquiries and a list of licensing authorities. Does not establish universal register coverage, free access to every function, a particular company's status or a contact's authority. No individual record query made.

2. UAE Government, Searching for a job
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/find-a-job/searching-for-a-job
   Official guidance, dated 10 November 2026 on the page. Supports licensed-agency checks through MOHRE, recruitment fees borne by employers, distinction from optional portal products and independent employer-careers routes. The article does not turn a suspicious approach into a named-company fraud finding.

3. UAE Government, Tips to stay safe from labour and visa fraud
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/Visa-information/tips-to-avoid-labour-and-visa-fraud
   Official guidance, dated 10 November 2026. Supports company/offer checking, recruitment-expense guidance and official enquiry routes. Its broad MOHRE wording is not treated as proof that the same inquiry covers every UAE employment relationship.

4. UAE Government, Recruiting in free zones
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business/doing-business-in-free-zones/recruiting-in-free-zones
   Official procedure/scope source, dated 27 October 2026. Describes authority-specific employment/permit procedures and distinct DIFC/ADGM frameworks. Supports identifying the relevant authority, not deciding an individual's legal jurisdiction.

5. MOHRE Inquiry Service
   https://inquiry.mohre.gov.ae/
   Also reached through https://inquiry.mohre.gov.ae/Home/ . Opened the public page, switched from Arabic to English and selected Enquiry for Job Offer. The visible form displayed Transaction No, Company No, From Date, To Date, a permit selector and captcha. Actual pixels inspected. No values entered, captcha submitted, search performed or result inspected. Link and description are limited to this observed public entry point.

6. MOHRE old offer/contract entry point
   https://receipts.mohre.gov.ae/OfferInquiry/Index?langid=2
   The guide's previous destination was reachable, but its default screen displayed View my contract. It showed transaction/passport/nationality/date-of-birth fields. Do not describe this as a dead link or assert one universal field set. Replaced the guide link with the explicitly selected public job-offer inquiry route. Neither screen's result flow was tested.

7. DHL Group, Security and Phishing Information
   https://careers.dhl.com/global/en/phishing-information-notice
   Employer's own recruitment-safety notice. Supports DHL-specific independent verification, official-careers route and no advance money/service-purchase request as part of its recruitment process. Not evidence of a current UAE vacancy, a particular sender's identity or every employer's communications policy. No partnership implied.

8. DHL hiring FAQs, reviewed for context
   https://careers.dhl.com/global/en/faqs
   Describes that employer's public search/application process. No application was made. Not used to promise identical account or confirmation behavior across employers.

9. UAE Government, Employment laws and regulations in the private sector
   https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/sector-of-employment/employment-in-the-private-sector/employment-laws-and-regulations-in-the-private-sector
   Official coverage source. Lists government/security/domestic-worker exclusions and domestic-work legislation. Used only to avoid treating one private-sector workflow as universal, not to provide a full legal assessment.

10. MOHRE official home
    https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/home.aspx
    Reachable official support/service starting point. No phone, email, response-time or case-outcome promise copied into the guide.

## Source conflict handled

The older UAE Government working-in-free-zones page at https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/sector-of-employment/working-in-free-zones uses broader wording about Labour Law coverage than the newer recruiting-in-free-zones page. The article does not try to reconcile the law itself. It tells readers to identify and contact the relevant authority and does not turn a missing MOHRE result into an authenticity or legality finding.

Search results also returned third-party field instructions that differed between service screens. They were not used as the source of form instructions. The public service itself was inspected instead.

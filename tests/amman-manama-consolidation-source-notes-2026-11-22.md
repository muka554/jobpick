# Amman and Manama consolidation: primary source ledger
Checked 22 November 2026. Public-source research for the city/country consolidation; no sign-in, submission or individual eligibility testing. Eight primary pages were checked before the proposal, with the additional checks below during drafting. Primary sources are appropriate for service scope; no market statistics or testimonials are being inferred.

## Jordan Ministry of Labour, Employment Directorate
URL: https://mol.gov.jo/EN/Pages/Employment_Directorate
Type: official government directorate description.
Supports: national employment-platform matching, career guidance/counselling, interview/CV/search-skills support and employer coordination. Establishes different public functions, not a guarantee of placement or individual access.
Confidence: high for the published functions.

## Sajjil homepage
URL: https://sajjil.gov.jo/
Type: official national employment platform, also identified by the Ministry.
Supports: individual job/training search and employer functions. Public root was readable. Does not establish access to an authenticated account or the current status of every advertised role.
Limits: visible sample entries carried dates; do not describe them as confirmed open roles or use displayed promotional CV counts.

## Jordan National Employment Programme
URL: https://www.sajjil.gov.jo/nep
Type: official programme description and criteria in Arabic.
Supports: distinct programme for employing Jordanian men/women in private enterprises, with separate jobseeker and enterprise criteria. At this check, the jobseeker criteria included Jordanian nationality, age 18-45, all governorates/education levels and at least a month unemployed. Those are this programme's public criteria, not a universal rule for all Sajjil activity or work-permit eligibility.
Limits: full terms, individual suitability, benefit approval and programme account not tested. Proposed copy should avoid benefit amounts, support periods and a condensed legal checklist unless needed and separately verified.

## Royal Hashemite Court, King Hussein Business Park
URL: https://rhc.jo/en/king-hussein-business-park
Type: official public project/place description.
Supports: business complex in Amman with local/international companies and start-ups. Useful employer-research example.
Does not support: a share of technology hiring, active vacancies, a company's recruitment intentions or a particular employee's worksite. Do not republish undated company/employment counts as current market data.

## Bahrain National Portal, jobseeker registration
URL: https://services.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BSP/GSX-UI-EServiceDetails?esID=2274
Type: official Ministry of Labour service description.
Supports: Bahraini nationality, minimum age 15, unemployment and listed exclusions/qualification evidence. Required attachments include photo, valid national ID, educational evidence, CV, IBAN and conditional additional evidence. These are public service instructions, not a request for JobPick to collect any of them.
Limits: no individual eligibility finding or authenticated submission. Preserve the guide's bounded wording and link the actual service.

## Tamkeen, National Employment Program
URL: https://tamkeen.bh/en/programs/national-employment-program-3-0/
Type: official programme operator description, criteria and FAQ.
Supports: enterprise wage-support programme for employing Bahraini nationals; enterprise and employee conditions both apply. FAQ says enterprises submit through Tamkeen's portal. A support programme is not universal jobseeker registration or a substitute for an employer application.
Limits: meeting initial criteria does not guarantee approval; terms and official-record checks apply. Do not present general-track wage thresholds as universal legal minimum wages. No company application or subsidy approval tested.

## Bahrain Airport Company careers
URL: https://www.bahrainairport.bh/corporate/careers
Type: official employer careers page.
Supports: BAC's managing/operating role, its own named business areas and a vacancy-list link.
Does not support: every airport vacancy being employed by BAC, generic-board coverage assertions, current suitable roles, or the actual worksite of a particular advert.

## Central Bank of Bahrain licensing
URL: https://www.cbb.gov.bh/licensing-registration/
Type: official regulator description.
Supports: licensing/registration scope for regulated services and the applicable-rule distinction. Existing country guide already bounds this correctly.
Limits: no individual employer lookup, current licence status, authenticity verdict for an advert or requirement that every finance-related employer hold an identical licence. Preserve that correction rather than broadening it.

# Country-guide distinctness remediation — source notes

**Research and rewrite date:** 27 August 2026

This record supports the rewrite of the Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Jordan, Egypt, and Kuwait country guides. The aim is to replace interchangeable regional copy with a separate, reader-useful decision on a public route, required evidence, or the difference between an announcement and an available service. Universal safety content was moved to the canonical [job application safety and evidence checklist](/guides/recruitment-scam-warning-signs/), rather than copied into every country guide.

| Guide | Source reviewed | Supportable page-specific point | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bahrain | [Bahrain Government — Register new job seekers and update data](https://services.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BSP/GSX-UI-EServiceDetails?esID=2274) | The service states conditions including Bahraini nationality, minimum age, unemployment, and restrictions related to being a student, employee, retired, or commercial-register owner. It lists education verification, a CV, ID, photo, and other attachments. | The guide does not determine a reader’s eligibility or whether the current page’s requirements will apply to an individual case. |
| Qatar | [Civil Service and Government Development Bureau — Kawader](https://cgb.gov.qa/En/Services/Pages/eService-Details.aspx?itemID=6) | Kawader’s published audience is Qataris and children of Qatari women; the page describes government-sector recruitment, electronic follow-up, and audience-specific documents. | The guide does not say Kawader is open to all job seekers or decide eligibility for a government or private role. |
| Oman | [Gov.om — Register as a job seeker or update your information](https://gov.om/en/w/register-job-seeker-s-data-in-manpower-records) | The Ministry of Labour service says it can register a job seeker or update work experience/contact information. Its page lists a driving licence where required for the job and stamped, attested certificates/work-experience evidence. | The guide does not decide whether documents are acceptable, need attestation, or establish individual service eligibility. |
| Jordan | [Jordan Ministry of Labour — Employment Directorate](https://mol.gov.jo/EN/Pages/Employment_Directorate) | The Directorate describes matching through Sajjil, job-search support, and career guidance/counselling. It says Sajjil helps job seekers access suitable opportunities in Jordan and abroad. | The guide treats matching or guidance as a starting route, not proof of candidacy, vacancy availability, or access. |
| Egypt | [Egypt State Information Service — Ministry of Labor platform announcement](https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/ministry-of-labor-to-launch-platform-connecting-job-seekers-with-employers/) | A 1 May 2026 notice reports the announced launch of a national digital platform intended to connect job seekers and employers within Egypt and abroad. | The guide distinguishes a dated announcement from a confirmed live service, vacancy, registration channel, or available feature. |
| Kuwait | [Kuwait Government Online — Central Registration for Job Seekers](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoenglish/Pages/eServices/CSC/RegisterForJob.aspx) | The official page is titled “Central Registration for Job Seekers,” identifying a public registration route. Its full content was not extractable in this research environment. | The guide intentionally does not repeat conditions, availability, registration windows, documents, or eligibility. It tells readers to verify the live service directly before relying on it. |

## Content-design rule applied

The revised pages share only operational architecture: clear title, byline, source-review module, corrections link, and scoped consent/ad metadata. Each body now has a different practical decision tool: Bahrain eligibility gate; Qatar audience gate; Oman evidence readiness; Jordan matching versus guidance; Egypt announcement versus available route; and Kuwait live-service verification. The updated guides each link to the single central application-safety guide rather than repeating a generic scam checklist.

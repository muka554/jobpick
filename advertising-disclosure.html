Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: August 17, 2026

How this Site is funded

Gulf Job Hub is free to use and is supported by advertising, primarily through Google AdSense. Ads may appear as display banners in clearly marked panels, and occasionally as a sponsored link within the search results board itself.

How sponsored links are marked

Any sponsored or paid placement mixed into the search board is marked with a red ● SPONSORED tag and the word "Advertisement," distinct from the teal "LIVE" and amber "VIA GOOGLE" markers used for genuine job-platform search links. Sponsored rows link to a third party and are not job listings.

No editorial influence

Advertisers do not influence which job platforms are listed, how they're categorized, or how search links are built. The list of job platforms reflects an editorial judgment about what's useful for job seekers in the UAE, independent of any advertising relationship.

Google AdSense

Ads served through Google AdSense may be personalized based on your browsing activity, unless you've opted out. See our Privacy Policy for how to manage ad personalization, and Google's own Advertising Policies for more detail.

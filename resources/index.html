Primary sources used in editorial updates

We use primary public-service sources where available to add context about official employment routes. These sources are not endorsements of commercial platforms and can change without notice.

  • UAE Government — Government jobs — The UAE Government page links to federal and emirate-level public employment portals. Public-sector routes can have separate account, eligibility, and document requirements.
  • GOV.SA — Apply for jobs on the Jadarat platform — Jadarat is an official service for job seekers to browse and apply for available roles. The official page explains its profile, search, and application steps and identifies its intended audience.
  • World Bank — Public Employment Services in Egypt — This background research describes public employment services in Egypt. It is useful context for understanding the employment-support landscape, not a substitute for current employer or government instructions.
  • Qatar Civil Service and Government Development Bureau — Kawader — Kawader is a government recruitment platform for Qataris and children of Qatari women. Its official service page lists audience, document, and eligibility information; do not assume it is open to every job seeker.
  • Kuwait Government Online — Central Registration for Job Seekers — Kuwait Government Online provides a central registration route for job seekers. Check the current service page for audience, registration, and document requirements before relying on it.
  • Gov.om — Register as a job seeker or update your information — Oman’s Ministry of Labour service allows job seekers to register or update work-experience information. The official page identifies supporting evidence that may be needed for some applications.
  • Bahrain Government — Register new job seekers and update data — Bahrain’s public service page sets out a registration route and eligibility conditions for job seekers. Treat those conditions as service-specific and confirm the current requirements directly.
  • Jordan Ministry of Labour — Employment Directorate — Jordan’s Employment Directorate describes the National Employment Platform (Sajjil), employment matching, and career-guidance services. The Ministry states that the service supports CV, interview, and job-search guidance as well as matching.

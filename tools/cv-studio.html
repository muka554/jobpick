Writing and format notes

This is a local, guided editor, not an AI rewriting service or a live subscription. The interface and generated letter scaffold are English. You may enter Arabic and use right-to-left layout; review mixed-language output yourself. Word and text always use a simple linear layout, without sidebars or timeline decoration. Word and PDF pagination may differ.

Our layout choices draw on Greenhouse's parsing guidance. That is one vendor's guidance, not an ATS certification. Our writing prompts follow the National Careers Service and cover-letter advice; these are writing principles, not UAE rules. The UAE Government recommends an honest CV.

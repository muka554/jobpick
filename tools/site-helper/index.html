How this helper works and its limits

This helper uses prepared answers and a local page catalogue, not an AI service or a live adviser.

No account, tracking or saved conversation. Your keywords stay in this page. No upload, AI calls, external analysis, cookies or browser storage. Use a short topic, not your CV, identity details, passwords or confidential records. Browser history, extensions, autofill and device software are outside this page's control. Pages you open next have their own privacy and processing behavior.

Try "cover letter", "interview", "salary", "login" or "Arabic". Search finds words in prepared help and page titles/descriptions. It does not understand an unrestricted question or inspect your account.

It can show prepared, source-linked instructions for our tools and help you find a relevant page. It cannot submit applications, sign in for you, fill personal achievements, review a CV, confirm a live vacancy, decide licensing or work eligibility, or promise a job or salary. It has no conversation memory and does not infer anything about you.

The standalone cover-letter checker, interview planner, role explorer and this helper use page-only processing. The older signed-in CV workspace is different: it uses an account, uploads and private server processing. Read its current notice before using it. Do not assume a privacy promise on one page applies to the entire website.

Prepared help is in English. The catalogue includes the site's Arabic and other language pages and preserves their page-language labels. Keyword matching is limited: a spelling variation or an unlisted synonym may return no result. Use the topic menu or browse below rather than treating an empty result as proof that a guide does not exist.

Method and review record

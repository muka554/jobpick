Linkable resources for responsible sharing

Organizations may reference JobPick’s recruitment scam warning signs, Gulf job-platform guide, source-review methodology, and live employer listings. Please preserve the original URL and describe the resource accurately. This is an invitation to cite useful work—not a request for artificial links.

We do not buy backlinks, publish undisclosed paid guest posts, use private blog networks, or promise rankings. Strong backlinks should come from genuine editorial usefulness, employer or education partnerships, public-resource citations, and data or methodology that others can verify.
